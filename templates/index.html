Larvicide Reference Dosages

Temephos (Abate 50% EC)

Dosage: 1ml per 1000 litres (1 ppm). Safe for domestic non-drinking water containers. Effective for 7 to 10 days.

Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis (BTI)

Dosage: 0.5g/m² surface area. Biological larvicide highly specific to mosquito larvae. Eco-friendly and non-toxic to fish.

Monomolecular Film (Aquatain AMF)

Dosage: 1ml/m². Forms a thin silicone barrier on the water surface preventing pupae from breathing.
