Mosquito Prevention Protocols
🚫

Do not allow stagnant water to collect

Mosquitoes lay eggs exclusively in stagnant water. Ensure proper drainage in pits, puddles, and low-lying ground.

🪣

Empty buckets and containers regularly

Empty, scrub, and dry buckets, pet water dishes, and open drums at least once every 5 days.

🛢️

Cover water storage tanks tightly

Fit tight lids or fine wire mesh over overhead tanks, cisterns, and rain-harvesting barrels to prevent mosquito entry.

🧹

Clean and unblock drains

Remove plastic waste, silt, and fallen leaves from storm drains and domestic drainage sumps.

🛞

Remove old tyres and plastic waste

Discard or cover discarded automobile tyres, coconut shells, and plastic bottles which collect rainwater.

🪴

Check air coolers and flower pot trays

Change desert cooler water weekly and empty runoff trays beneath potted plants regularly.

⚡

Take immediate action when alert is generated

Inspect detected risk zone, activate the relay drainage pump, and apply eco-friendly larvicide (e.g. Abate/BTI).
